IMPORTANT DISTINCTION
Azure Pricing Calculator:
- forward-looking full-month estimate
- based on simplified assumptions

Azure Cost Management:
- actual subscription cost/usage data
- source of truth for real spend

The estimate was intentionally higher than actual captured cost because:
- the environment was short-lived
- resources were deleted/recreated
- current Dev was used only for evidence capture
- the calculator modeled a full-month baseline
- Cost Management reflected partial-month usage
- telemetry/load-testing cost depends on actual usage
